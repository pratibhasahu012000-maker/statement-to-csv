How it works

1. Private by design

The PDF is read by your browser with PDF.js. No server, no storage, no account.

2. Balance check

Each row's amount is checked against the running balance, so missed or misread lines stand out.

3. Any ledger

Plain CSV works with Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB, Sage or Excel.

Limits: works with text-based PDFs (most bank downloads). Scanned images need OCR, which isn't supported yet. Layouts vary by bank, so always review before import.
